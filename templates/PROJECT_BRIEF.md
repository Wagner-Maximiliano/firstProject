# PROJECT BRIEF

**The AMA agents read this file FIRST.** This brief is your project's instruction set—separate from the AMA framework itself. Fill it in once, and the framework will use it to guide all planning and building.

---

## Project Name

What is this project called?

---

## What You Want to Build

In plain language (not technical jargon): what are you building? Describe it as if you were explaining it to a friend.

**Example:** "A mobile app for tracking daily water intake and reminding people to drink more."

---

## Who It's For

Who will use this? What's their situation?

**Example:** "People trying to improve their hydration; fitness enthusiasts; people on medical advice to drink more water."

---

## Must-Haves

What absolutely has to be in v1? (List 3–5 core features or outcomes.)

---

## Nice-to-Haves

What would be cool but isn't essential? (These are candidates for v2 or deferral.)

---

## Things to Avoid / Out of Scope

Explicitly say what you do NOT want to build or take on. (This prevents scope creep.)

**Examples:**
- No social sharing / gamification in v1
- Not a replacement for a medical app
- No mobile apps in v1 (web only)

---

## Constraints

What limits do you have? (Pick one or more.)

- **Budget**: $ amount or "you decide"
- **Time**: deadline or "you decide"
- **Team size**: solo / small team / large team
- **Tech stack preference**: "Python + React", "Node + Vue", "use what makes sense", etc.
- **Other**: hosting choice, third-party service limitations, etc.

---

## Data & Privacy Notes

Does this project collect, store, or process any personal data?

- If yes: what data? How sensitive? Any compliance requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)?
- If no: say so.

---

## Git Branch

What branch should the AMA agents work on? (Never `main` or `master`.)

**Example:** `claude/build-project-v1`

---

## What "Done" Looks Like

How will you know the project is complete? What does go-live or handoff look like?

**Examples:**
- "Deployed to production and the user can sign up and log water intake."
- "Code is merged to main, tests pass, and documentation is written."
- "A working prototype I can click through on my phone."

---

## Anything Else?

Assumptions you're making, concerns, preferences, or context that helps the agents understand your world.

**Examples:**
- "I have a Figma design already."
- "This is a proof-of-concept; we might pivot."
- "We must use PostgreSQL for compliance reasons."
